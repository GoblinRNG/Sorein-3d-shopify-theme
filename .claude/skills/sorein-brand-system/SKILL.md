# Sorein Brand System

## Purpose
Use this skill whenever building, editing, reviewing, or writing anything for Sorein. This skill enforces the Sorein brand identity: premium Scandinavian energy-tech, futuristic but trustworthy, conversion-focused, and visually superior to ordinary Shopify stores.

## When to use
Use this skill for:
- Shopify theme design
- Homepage sections
- Product pages
- Collection pages
- Product builder UI
- Cart UI
- Brand copy
- Product education sections
- Ad landing pages
- Visual direction
- Design reviews
- UI upgrades

## Brand identity
Sorein is a premium energy-tech ecommerce brand selling:
- Portable power stations
- Solar generators
- Home backup systems
- Off-grid battery systems
- Modular power stations
- Solar panels
- RV/camping/emergency energy products

The brand should feel:
- Premium
- Scandinavian
- Clean
- Technical
- Calm
- Futuristic
- Trustworthy
- Powerful
- High-end
- Precise
- Market-dominating

## Visual language
Use:
- White studio backgrounds
- Soft grey surfaces
- Graphite technical panels
- Black premium contrast sections
- Cold blue, teal, cyan, and subtle green energy accents
- Soft shadows
- Large rounded corners
- Fine border lines
- Glass only when subtle
- Blueprint-style overlays
- Technical grid systems
- Product specification callouts
- Premium product photography
- Clean iconography
- Generous spacing
- Clear hierarchy

Avoid:
- Cheap neon gaming style
- Purple generic SaaS gradients
- Random AI-looking backgrounds
- Low-resolution product images
- Overcrowded product cards
- Confusing animations
- Dropshipping-style trust badges
- Fake scarcity
- Aggressive red sale spam
- Inconsistent typography
- Messy mobile layouts

## Typography direction
Use modern, clean, premium typography:
- Large calm hero headlines
- Short powerful subheadlines
- Technical but readable body copy
- Strong product names
- Specification tables that are easy to scan
- Clear CTA labels
- Minimal decorative text

Headline tone:
- Confident
- Calm
- Premium
- Technical
- Benefit-led

Example tone:
- "Power your home when the grid stops."
- "Modular backup energy for cabins, homes, and off-grid life."
- "Built for serious energy independence."
- "Clean power. Expandable capacity. Real backup confidence."

Avoid:
- Hype-only text
- Fake revolutionary claims
- Overused AI phrases
- Too much exclamation
- Cheap discount language

## Layout principles
Every page should feel like a premium product launch page mixed with a high-converting ecommerce store.

Use:
- Strong hero section
- Clear product story
- Clear use cases
- Technical proof
- Trust blocks
- Comparison blocks
- Modular product systems
- Sticky buying CTAs
- Clean mobile flow
- Smooth section transitions

## Sorein design benchmark
The site should feel more premium and futuristic than typical power station brands. It should look like a serious energy-tech company, not a dropshipping store.

Before finalizing any Sorein design, check:
- Does it look expensive?
- Is it easy to buy?
- Is the product clearly explained?
- Does the layout feel trustworthy for 25–65 year old buyers?
- Does mobile feel polished?
- Are animations useful, not distracting?
- Would this compete against EcoFlow-level ecommerce?
